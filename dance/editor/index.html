map of the composition (click an element to select its steps)

    click a step; shift-click extends the range; up/down move the selection by one (wrapping); shift+up/down grow it at that edge, ctrl+shift+up/down shrink it there. On the floor: left-drag moves a selected foot (1 cm), right-drag rotates it (5 deg, hold alt for 1 deg); ctrl-z undoes; wheel zooms, dragging empty floor pans. Arrows show where each foot faces. Faint grey = where every foot stands before the first selected step; the start-state feet are outlined. "play" loops the element or composition on the floor with the music (no ffmpeg): the music runs on and the dance continues from where the dancers are, padded with triple steps (marked as filler) when it would not loop on its own; an element that ends in another position restarts from its start; "render video" makes an mp4. An element has one skeleton per starting condition (state and free foot): the dropdown picks it. Every edit autosaves within a second. Compositions are lists of entries: an element, a sub-composition (a named list of elements, e.g. RTT), or either "x N"; each entry's starting condition is dictated by the previous one and the add list offers only what can follow.
