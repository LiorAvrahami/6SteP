## Q1. What is the "rock back cutoff"?

Asked: the closest match in the lurklurk notes is **Closed Changing Places** (`m_closed_changing_places`):
rock step; slight forward triple turning 90 right on count 4 (the leader steps forward and to his right
across the follower's path, she forward and left across his); back step turning another 90 on 5; close;
slight back step on 6. The couple ends on a line translated forward, facing the other way. In the notes the
follower ends on the leader's **left**; the user's description ("rotated by 180 degrees") could instead mean
she stays on his right. Which is it?

**Answer: "yes this is it, and yes they end on the left like you said."** So the cutoff ends in a reversed
side position: side by side, both facing the same way, the follower on the leader's LEFT, his left arm around
her back. The second six beats of the same lurklurk page (mirror image: leader turns left, follower right)
bring her back to his right.

## Q2. Follower underarm pass from side by side

Asked: the notes have no such move. The agent's guess was that she passes in front of the leader from his
right to his left under his raised left hand, turning 270 anticlockwise, and backs away, while he turns 90
anticlockwise in place. One sentence from the user replaces the guess.

**Answer:** "she turns somewhere between 180 and 270: if we start parallel then she turns 180, if we start
facing each other (which we don't usually do) she turns 270. Basically she simply goes under the arm and
rotates **clockwise** in triple steps."

So from side by side: after the rock step, she goes under the raised left arm and turns clockwise 180 in the
two triple steps, ending in front of the leader facing him; the leader stays put. From facing each other
(rare for them) she would turn clockwise 270.

## Q3. Lead underarm pass

Asked: built as the user's "Changing Places with the roles mirrored" (the leader takes the follower's
travelling path under the joined hands, she takes his small steps). The notes' alternative is the leader
turning under his own arm on 5&6 after she has passed. Which one?

**Answer:** "actually I turn **180 left**, but yes I always go on her **right side** I think." So: the leader
goes under the joined hands on the follower's right side turning 180 left; she takes the small steps; places
swapped.

## Q4. Where does the circle end?

Asked: the notes' rotation totals for the Lindy Circle (leader 360, follower 540) only close up if the circle

easily fixable by just making one of us rotate less or more" (rotation amounts may be adjusted).

## Q7. Opacity of a foot that carries no weight

Asked: opacity equal to the weight makes the free foot invisible while it travels; a faint minimum of about
0.2 was proposed.

**Answer:** "I don't like that. Make two colours on top of each other: a **grey baseline with opacity 0.2
below the colour of the foot**." So every foot is a grey shape at 0.2, with the coloured foot drawn on top
at an opacity equal to its weight.
