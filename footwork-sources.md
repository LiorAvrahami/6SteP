# Footwork notes for the feet animation

Written 2026-10-16. Section 1 is Claude's prior intuition, written BEFORE reading any sources, so it can be graded. Section 2 is filled from the web research. Section 3 is the consensus the animation uses.

Conventions: counts are the dancer's counts (1-8), "&" is the swung middle of a triple step (lands about 2/3 through the beat). "In place" means weight change without travel. Leader's left is the follower's right when facing each other.

## 1. Claude's intuition (unverified)

### 6-count basic, closed position
Identical to the user's description. Leader: 1 rock back L, 2 replace R, 3&4 triple L-R-L (first step comes forward to home), 5&6 triple R-L-R in place. Follower mirrors: 1 rock back R, 2 replace L, 3&4 triple R-L-R, 5&6 triple L-R-L.
Confidence: high. Note: many East Coast Swing schools teach the same loop starting on the triples (triple, triple, rock step). It is the same cycle shifted by two counts. Lindy schools usually count it rock step first.

### 8-count basic in place (the "Lindy basic" / groove walk)
Leader: 1 rock back L, 2 replace R, 3&4 triple L-R-L, 5 step R, 6 step L, 7&8 triple R-L-R. Follower mirrors. Confidence: high for the sequence, medium for how far the walks travel (in place when practising, travelling in the swingout).

### Swingout from open position (8 counts)
Leader: 1 rock step back on L, slightly angled, 2 replace R, 3&4 triple L-R-L travelling forward and turning about a quarter to the left so the follower passes on the leader's right side, 5 step R backward-ish while pivoting, 6 step L continuing the pivot so the leader has turned about half a turn by the end of 6, 7&8 triple R-L-R moving slightly back, sending the follower out. Net: the leader has rotated 180 degrees and ended roughly where the follower started, on the slot's far side.
Follower: 1 rock step back on R, 2 replace L, 3&4 triple R-L-R travelling forward toward and past the leader, 5 L step forward, 6 R step with a half pivot (the follower turns so her back is to where she came from, about 180 degrees across 5-6), 7&8 triple L-R-L travelling backward out of the slot.
Confidence: medium. Known uncertainty: whether the follower's count 1 is a true back step or more of a weight change in place, and how the 180 degrees is split between 3&4 and 5-6 (I believe most of the turning is on 5-6).

### Lindy circle (swingout from closed that stays closed)
Same footwork and counts as the swingout, both partners rotate 360 degrees around a shared centre over the 8 counts, staying in closed position.

### Charleston basic (8 counts, side by side or facing)
Leader: 1 rock back L, 2 replace R, 3 kick L forward, 4 step L down, 5 kick R forward, 6 step R back, 7 kick L back, 8 step L. Follower mirrored (rock back on R). Confidence: low-medium on the exact kick/step order for counts 5-8; the general shape (back, replace, kick forward, step, kick forward, step back, kick back, step) is how I remember it taught.

### Tuck turn (6 counts)
Leader: 1-2 rock step, 3&4 triple while compressing the follower in, 5&6 triple while releasing her into a right turn. Follower: 1-2 rock step, 3&4 triple in, 5&6 triple turning right (clockwise) under the arm.

### 6-count closed basic (lurklurk Closed Basic, corroborated by Wikipedia and Wikibooks)
| Count | Leader | Follower |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | L back, weight L, left hand pushes slightly | R back, weight R, turning slightly right |
| 2 | replace weight on R in place | replace L, turning slightly left to face the leader |
| 3 | L small step to the side | R small step to the side |
| & | R closes to L | L closes to R |
| 4 | L small step to the side | R small step to the side |
| 5 | R small step to the side | L small step to the side |
| & | L closes to R | R closes to L |
| 6 | R small step to the side | L small step to the side |

Notes: rock step on 1-2 in all Lindy sources; the ECS "triple, triple, rock step" count is the same loop shifted by two. No source gives a leader rotation for the closed basic; the usual slow clockwise drift is common practice, not sourced. Early single-step version (Wikipedia): 1 L back, 2 R replace, 3 L side, 5 R side.

### Swingout from closed (lurklurk Swing Out)
| Count | Leader | Follower |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | L back, weight L (rock) | R back, weight R, opening the position |
| 2 | replace R, slight turn right | replace L |
| 3 | L side, turning about 90 right | R slightly forward |
| & | R closes to L, slight right | replace L |
| 4 | L side; right hand pulls follower forward then releases | R forward |
| 5 | R back behind L (hook), 90 right, clearing the follower's path | L forward, turning 90 right |
| 6 | L side, another 90 right | R back (or cross R in front of L), another 90 right |
| 7 | R slightly back | L slightly back |
| & | L closes to R | R closes to L |
| 8 | replace R | replace L |

### Swingout from open (lurklurk Lindy Turn)
| Count | Leader | Follower |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | L back, weight L, J-shaped pull with the left hand | R slightly forward (twist step) |
| 2 | replace forward onto R | L forward (twist step) |
| 3 | L forward; right hand to the follower's back | R forward; left hand to the leader's right shoulder |
| & | R closes to L, slight right | L closes to R, slight right |
| 4 | L forward, slight right; by the end of 4 the couple faces opposite to the start (about 180) | R forward, slight right |
| 5 | R back behind L, 90 right; pulls with right hand then releases | L forward, 90 right, now facing partner |
| 6 | L side, 90 right | R back behind L (or cross in front), 90 right |
| 7 | R slightly back | L slightly back |
| & | L closes to R | R closes to L |
| 8 | replace R | replace L |

Rotation: the old Wikipedia text says the leader turns 90 degrees per two counts, 360 per swingout, and the follower travels a full circle around him. lurklurk agrees for the Lindy Turn (about 180 by the end of 4, plus 90 on 5 and 90 on 6). The follower's own "turn back to face the leader" is the 180 that people quote, done on 6 or late on 8 depending on style. Wikibooks' follower triple on 3&4 is written L-R-L, almost certainly a typo for R-L-R.

Lindy Circle: same as the Lindy Turn on 1-4, then 5 leader R slightly forward between the follower's feet 90 right / follower L diagonally forward 90 right; 6 leader L side 90 right / follower R crosses in front 90 right; 7 leader R back / follower L back, 90 right; & closes; 8 slight back. Swing Out in Place = first half of the closed swingout plus the second half of the Lindy Circle.

### Side-by-side Charleston, 8 counts (lurklurk, Wikibooks, Reading Museum agree)
| Count | Leader | Follower |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | L back, weight L | R back, weight R |
| 2 | replace R forward | replace L |
| 3 | kick L forward, no weight | kick R forward |
| 4 | L small step forward, weight L | R small step forward |
| 5 | kick R forward | kick L forward |
| 6 | hitch: lift R beside L, knee bent, no weight | hitch L |
| 7 | kick R back | kick L back |
| 8 | R small step back, weight R | L small step back |

Variant (Reading Museum): 5-6 become two weight changes in place instead of kick-hitch-kick. Tandem Charleston (lurklurk Back Charleston, both on the same feet): 1 point L back, 2 hitch L, 3 kick L forward, 4 L closes with weight, 5 kick R forward, 6 hitch R, 7 kick R back, 8 R closes with weight.

### Tuck turn (lurklurk Turn Under, closed to open)
| Count | Leader | Follower |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | L back, left hand pushes | R back, slight right |
| 2 | replace R | replace L, slight left |
| 3 | L small side, left hand lifting | R small side |
| & | R closes to L | L closes to R |
| 4 | L small side, left hand above the follower's right shoulder | R small side |
| 5 | weight to R in place, left hand pushes out to turn her, right hand releases | L crosses in front of R, turning 180 right |
| & | L closes to R | R closes to L, a further 90 right |
| 6 | weight to R | replace L, final 90 right (360 total), facing the leader in open |

Sugar push (lurklurk, West Coast style): leader 1 L back, 2 R closes, 3 L hook behind R, & replace R, 4 L closes, 5 R hook behind L, & L slightly forward, 6 R slightly forward; follower 1 R forward, 2 L closes, 3 R hook behind, & replace L, 4 R closes, 5 L hook behind, & R slightly back, 6 L slightly back.

### Grading Claude's section 1 against the sources
- 6-count basic: sequence and feet correct. Wrong detail: the sources' triples are small SIDE steps with a close on the "&" (side-close-side), not a forward step then steps in place. The user's "forward" first triple is a reasonable recovery from the rock, but the written form is side-close-side.
- 8-count basic in place: matches the rhythm pages. Not described as a standalone move by lurklurk, which treats the 8-count rhythm directly through the swingout.
- Swingout from open: the sequence, feet, and most-of-the-turn-on-5-6 were right. Wrong: the total rotation is about 360 degrees per swingout with both partners returning to their starting spots, not 180, and the follower's 1-2 is a forward twist step, not a rock step back. The leader's count 5 is specifically a hook step behind the left foot.
- Lindy circle: right in spirit (same footwork, stays closed), and the 360 was right here.
- Charleston: my low-confidence guess had the right shape on 1-5 but put a step on 6. The sources have a weightless hitch on 6, kick back on 7, step back on 8, so 7-8 were right and 6 was wrong.
- Tuck turn: correct skeleton. The sources add that the follower's turn is 360 (180 on 5, 90 on &, 90 on 6) with a cross step on 5.

### Position and hand connection (lurklurk Dance Positions, Hand Holds, Lead and Follow)
- Closed: face to face. Leader's right hand on the follower's left shoulder blade, his left hand holding her right hand at shoulder height out to his left, her left hand on his right shoulder.
- Side: follower on the leader's right, both facing the same direction. His right arm around her back, her left hand on his right shoulder. **This is how the user dances the basic**, so the animation defaults to side-by-side for the basic. Footwork is unchanged: both rock back on count 1, feet mirrored.
- Open: facing, a short distance apart, connected only by his left hand to her right hand at about waist height, elbows slightly bent, springy tension.
- Grip: his left hand thumb up, index and middle fingers bent to point right, other fingers folded; her right hand palm down, fingers curled around his two fingers; no thumb grip.
- Swingout from open: only the hand hold on 1-2; on 3 his right hand goes to her back and her left hand to his shoulder (closed frame); he pulls with the right hand on 4-5 and releases immediately after 5; hand hold only from 5 to 8. Leads: push or pull at waist height for direction; raise her right hand above her right shoulder and push for a clockwise turn; raise it in front of her face for anticlockwise; in closed, lifting one side of her body with the right arm puts her weight on the other foot.

## 3. Consensus to use
- Counts and feet from lurklurk, which is internally consistent for leader and follower in every move checked.
- Triple-step "&" at about 2/3 of the beat, matching the triplet swing measured in the music (offbeat at 0.67-0.71 of the beat).
- Weight transfers fully on every step, close and replace; kicks, hitches, points and touches carry no weight.
- Rotation per step as lurklurk writes it (multiples of 45 degrees).
